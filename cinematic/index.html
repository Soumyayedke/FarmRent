A tractor sits idle for most of the year.Owners paid for the machine and it earns nothing between seasons.

A field waits on one for a single week.Smallholder farmers can’t justify buying equipment they need for days, so the work is late or done by hand.

FarmRent puts the two on the same road.Local farmers find nearby owners, request dates and agree a cost before anything moves.

Two people, one booking

For farmers

  1. Browse nearby equipmentFilter by machine type and see the daily price and specs.
  2. Request your datesThe day count and total update as you pick dates. You see the cost before you send.
  3. Wait for approval, then workThe owner accepts or declines. Track every request from your dashboard.
  4. Pay and keep a receiptCheckout is a clearly labelled demo for now: no real money moves.

For equipment owners

  1. List your machineAdd the type, daily price and specs once.
  2. Review each requestSee the farmer’s dates and accept or decline.
  3. Mark the job completeApproval and payment are tracked as separate things, so nothing is assumed.
  4. See what you earnYour dashboard shows earnings from completed rentals.

Availability can be switched on or off any time.
